About

I am a second-year PhD student in Computer Science at New York University, advised by Qi Sun. I work on efficient neural representations for visual computing and graphics with interests in compression, generation, and reconstruction.

Before my PhD, I completed my BS in computer science and cognitive science at the University of Richmond, where I worked with Arryn Robbins and Catherine Finegan-Dollak on webcam eye tracking and visual search.
